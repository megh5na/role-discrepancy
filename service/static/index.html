    Known limits

    • Benign documents that quote a request are flagged 93 to 99% of the time, by every scorer we tried.
    • False-alarm rates on benign role-mixed text range from 0.18 to 0.83 depending on the cell.
    • Transplant is robust to carrier formatting but is not the better detector. At the document threshold the naive ensemble flags 83% of attacks and the transplant ensemble 58%.
    • Tool and reasoning channels have no reliable threshold, so no verdict is shown for them.
